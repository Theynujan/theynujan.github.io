About

I recently completed a B.Sc. (Hons) in Surveying Sciences at Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka, specialising in Hydrographic Surveying. The programme is recognised by the IBSC at FIG/IHO/ICA Category B level.

My field work covers both water and land: single-beam echosounder and side-scan sonar surveys, sound velocity profiling, topographic and control surveys, and UAV mapping. I process that data in HYPACK, Civil 3D, ArcGIS Pro, QGIS and Bentley ContextCapture.

Since July 2026 I have been a Hydrographic and Land Surveying Intern at the National Aquatic Resources Research and Development Agency (NARA), working on coastal and shallow-water surveys in the north of Sri Lanka. My final-year research applied YOLOv11 to detect subsea pipelines and communication cables in side-scan sonar imagery, which is where my interest in machine learning for survey data comes from.

I am looking for hydrographic and land surveying positions where I can keep building field and data-processing experience.
